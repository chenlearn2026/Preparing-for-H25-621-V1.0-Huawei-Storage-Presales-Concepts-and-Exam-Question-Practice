# Preparing-for-H25-621-V1.0-Huawei-Storage-Presales-Concepts-and-Exam-Question-Practice
Storage presales discussions connect a customer's requirements with the architecture and capabilities of a proposed solution. The HCSP-Presales-Storage V1.0 examination assesses knowledge of Huawei storage products and solutions for that work.
H25-621 is the official examination code; H25-621_V1.0 identifies the code together with its version.

## Establish the examination context

The associated credential is HCSP-Presales-Storage, expanded as Huawei Certified Specialist Professional-Presales-Storage. Huawei identifies partner sales professionals selling its storage products and certification candidates as the intended audience.

- Official examination code: H25-621.
- Version: V1.0.
- Duration: 90 minutes.
- Assessment type: written examination.
- Formats: single-answer, multiple-answer and true/false questions.
- Passing score: 600 out of 1000.
- Listed examination cost: 100 USD.
- Language: English.

The score threshold is not a published raw correct-answer percentage. The reviewed materials do not establish an exact question count. Confirm current registration conditions before scheduling.

The page identifies corresponding Position-based Learning, Hands-on Skills and Project practices as prerequisites. Keep those requirements separate from the examination topics when planning preparation.

## Understand the seven weighted areas

DCS accounts for 8%. Study the full-stack datacenter solution overview and the main features of eSphere and eDME. Explain how the named components fit the solution rather than treating DCS as another storage-product label.

DME accounts for 7%. Its detailed topics include the data-management solution, DMS introduction, DME IQ, DME features and customer cases. Preserve the distinctions between these terms in your notes.

OceanProtect Backup Storage accounts for 12%. Review backup-storage architecture, data reduction, performance, availability and best practices with backup software. The guide also includes the backup appliance and its architecture and best practice.

OceanStor Dorado accounts for 28%. Cover the overview, hardware/software architecture, Hyper-series features, Smart-series features and other key capabilities.

OceanStor Pacific accounts for 20%. Topics include architecture, performance, reliability, efficiency, security/stability and typical scenarios.

POC accounts for 8%. Study test design, recommended flash-storage content, performance and reliability precautions, OceanProtect tests, DME and scale-out considerations.

Reliability accounts for 17%. Review metrics and module, system, datacenter and O&M reliability, together with tests and certifications.

## Prepare through requirements and architecture notes

Start with a fictional customer requirement and identify which information you would need before discussing a solution. Separate confirmed requirements from assumptions, especially where performance, backup or availability is involved.

Use Huawei courseware and the official exam outline to develop your notes. The outline is general guidance and says other content may also appear. A short bullet does not establish a complete implementation procedure.

Create a comparison between Dorado and Pacific using documented architecture and scenario information. Record why a capability matters to the fictional requirement without inventing supported capacities, benchmarks or universal product recommendations.

Use authorized training environments for practical work. Keep actual customer configurations, credentials and confidential test results out of shared study material.

## Turn exam questions into targeted review

After selecting an answer, explain why it fits the requested outcome. Compare it with another plausible choice and identify the condition that separates them.

Organize errors by domain and cause. Confusing DME with eDME needs terminology review; misunderstanding a POC precaution needs study of the testing requirement. Give each error a specific correction.

Keep correct guesses in the same review log. Return to those questions after a break, then test the concept with a different fictional requirement. Practice scores should guide revision rather than predict an examination result.

## Connect certification preparation with presales responsibilities

HCSP-Presales-Storage focuses on understanding Huawei's storage portfolio and holding detailed technical discussions with customers. Architecture, backup, reliability and POC knowledge support that goal.

The credential provides a defined learning target for presales professionals. It does not replace experience, stakeholder discovery or a properly designed proof of concept. Avoid presenting a certification as a guarantee of career progression or customer outcomes.

Good preparation should leave you able to distinguish a documented product feature from a claim that still needs investigation. That distinction is useful in both scenario questions and customer discussions.

## Finish with a balanced revision sequence

First establish the storage foundation: SAN, NAS, object storage, redundancy, backup and disaster recovery concepts. Next, work through the seven weighted domains, giving Dorado and Pacific suitable attention without overlooking smaller areas.

Follow with requirement-based examples and a review of decisions you cannot yet justify. Finish with mixed question practice and a check of current Huawei instructions, prerequisite activities and registration details.
